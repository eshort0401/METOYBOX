Land-Sea Breeze
===========================================

Land-sea breeze models are special both scientifically, and to me personally. `Rotunno's 1983 paper`_ on the linear theory of the land-sea breeze was the first atmospheric science paper I read after switching from math to meteorology. After 10 years, I am still thinking about it.

.. _Rotunno's 1983 paper: https://doi.org/10.1175/1520-0469(1983)040<1999:OTLTOT>2.0.CO;2
